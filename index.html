Go-to-market · commercial strategy

AI agents run the top of the funnel.

MHO Saudi does not buy its pipeline. In the UAE, an autonomous agent runs search — researching, writing, publishing and auditing every day — and the enquiries arrive inbound. That engine ports to the Kingdom on day one: same agent, Arabic corpus, Saudi intent. Intersect's BD network opens the enterprise doors on top, where a single group at INNOVO / ASGC scale is 25–30k employees.

01 Generate · the agent

An AI agent publishes to the live site every day, unattended, and audits the technical surface as it goes — indexation, titles, internal links, broken conversion paths. In the UAE it has built a 74-article corpus across ~105 indexed pages. No media buy: the cost is agent runtime, which is why year-one marketing sits at ~$30k.

02 Target · intent, not keywords

The agent aims at the moment of decision, not at traffic. Three intent classes: creation (new office, relocation, fit-out), obligation (what labour law actually requires in a pantry), cost (what a company should budget, how to run the RFP) — each cluster internally linked so one query pulls a buyer down the path. For KSA it re-points at Saudi intent: Arabic-first, Riyadh / Jeddah / Dammam geo pages, Saudi labour-law and Vision-2030 localisation angles.

03 Convert & expand

Benchmarking is the wedge: we show a prospect their current spend against the MHO benchmark and quantify the saving — UAE clients cut ≈22%. The platform then removes procurement friction (real-time cost tracking, auto-reordering, eco-smart picks) and makes the account sticky: 98% retention. We land one office and expand across the group.
